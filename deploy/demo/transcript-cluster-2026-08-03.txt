D — B-002 measured INSIDE the container, not on the host
==============================================================================

policy/policy.example.yaml was authored on a case-INsensitive macOS volume where
/var and /tmp carry a /private indirection; it is loaded in a case-sensitive
Linux container where they do not. The loader does not canonicalize prefixes, so
a prefix can mean one thing where it was written and another where it is used.
Every shipped prefix here is /workspace/-rooted, so the trap should be dormant —
this is the measurement that says so, taken in the pod.

  canonical_path('/workspace/notes.txt')
    -> '/workspace/notes.txt'   (unchanged)
    still under the policy prefix '/workspace/': True
  canonical_path('/workspace/.ssh/id_rsa')
    -> '/workspace/.ssh/id_rsa'   (unchanged)
    still under the policy prefix '/workspace/': True

  Equal to the input is what makes the asymmetry benign FOR THIS POLICY IN THIS
  IMAGE. It is not a general claim about B-002, which is still open.
